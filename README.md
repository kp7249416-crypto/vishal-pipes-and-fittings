# Vishal Pipes and Fittings – Agricultural Irrigation Solutions

Vishal Pipes and Fittings provides piping solutions for agricultural irrigation and water management applications.

## Our Products

- HDPE Pipes
- Sprinkler Pipes
- Rain Pipes
- Suction Pipes
- Lay Flat Pipes
- Column Pipes
- Lateral and Mini Sprinkler Pipes

## Choosing Pipes for Agriculture

Selecting the right pipe depends on the irrigation method, required water flow, operating pressure, field size and installation conditions. Farmers should check the pipe specifications and suitability before purchasing.
